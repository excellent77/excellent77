## Featured Projects

### [UEO-AI — Universal Energy Operation Optimization](https://github.com/excellent77/UEO_AI)

**Python · PyTorch · LSTM/GRU/Transformer · Soft Actor-Critic · Gradio**

An end-to-end industrial AI research prototype that learns equipment dynamics from historical time-series data and converts them into operating recommendations.

I developed a configurable workflow covering:

`Data Upload > Preprocessing > Environment Modeling > RL Optimization > Inference`

The framework combines **LSTM/GRU/Transformer environment models** with a **Soft Actor-Critic policy** and provides an interactive Gradio interface for model training, optimization, and recommendation export.

---

### [School Bus Routing — Exact Optimization vs. Scalable Heuristics](https://github.com/excellent77/SBRP_algorithms)

**Python · Gurobi · Mixed-Integer Programming · Column Generation · ACO · GA · LNS**

An operations research project studying multi-school bus routing while balancing **operational efficiency and student ride fairness**.

I implemented and compared exact optimization, Dantzig-Wolfe decomposition, column generation, and multiple metaheuristics across problem sizes of **30, 50, and 80 stops**.

The project explores a question that how can we preserve decision quality and explicit constraints while scaling optimization to larger real-world problems?

### [Medical Image Segmentation](https://github.com/excellent77/medical-image-segmentation)

**Python · PyTorch · MobileUNETR · U-Net · MONAI · Albumentations · W&B**

## Current Research

### Smart Engineering BOM Generation

I am currently developing an undergraduate capstone system that combines **RT-DETR + SAHI** with **DXF Vector Parsing + KD-Tree + Graph-Based Topology Reconstruction** to extract components and structural relationships from industrial piping drawings and generate structured **Bill of Materials (BOM)** information.

The project explores how **probabilistic computer vision and deterministic engineering geometry** can complement each other, with Human-in-the-Loop verification when their outputs disagree.

**Status:** Ongoing research; code and industrial data are not yet publicly released.
